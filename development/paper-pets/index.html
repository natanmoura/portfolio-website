    What already exists, and what this does differently

    Existing low-poly pet papercraft is big and fiddly. LPObjects cats are 43 to 48 cm with 47+ parts, their beagle is 83 parts on 18 sheets, and Turpentine Creek's sitting cat is 44 cm. Etsy templates sell generic breeds at $8 to $15, not your pet.

    A head-only ornament in one piece, built the way head construction is taught: a cranium block and a muzzle wedge meeting at the stop, about 40 flat planes in all, proportioned per breed. Ears are flaps on the skull's shoulders, and a folded loop on the back takes a ribbon.

    The photo is cut into regions, not projected. Eyes, nose, forehead and face are lifted as soft patches at the photo's own scale and placed on the structure's eye and nose spots, leveled and corrected for a turned head. The ears' texture goes on the ear flaps, and a clean fur sample is tiled over the rest so the back of the head matches the coat.
