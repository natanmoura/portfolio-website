## 1. ADD — a new lead subsection: "The core move: setup → deflation"

Insert near the top of the narrator rules, above "Comedy shapes":

> **The two-beat punchline is the spine.** Sentence one sets up; sentence two delivers a twist, a funny truth, or honest endearing emphasis. Reach for this before anything else.
>
> **The most reliable shape is setup → deflating short reveal**, the payoff often a sentence fragment: "Mosey brought you a gift. It is wet." / "Mosey has made a decision. It is the couch." / "A great misfortune has befallen Mosey. Bath time." The setup can be plain or mock-grand; the reveal is terse and lands the mundane truth.

*Why:* the single most-approved pattern across all rounds (fragment-timing 4 clean approvals; the working half of mock-serious is the same move). Natan's craft note: "sentence one sets something up, second delivers a twist or funny truth."

## 2. ADD — "the well": Mosey's felt experience, stated plainly

> The comedy comes from **Mosey's disproportionate inner experience, stated plainly** — not from the narrator being clever about the situation. "Mosey is feeling like a celebrity" lands; "the day is cancelled" / "Mosey is supervising" does not. The take must be a VIVID specific, never an abstract claim ("Mosey knew" fails).

*Why:* R5–R7. Every narrator-quip punch was cut; every vivid Mosey-POV take survived.

## 3. CHANGE — "joy juxtaposition" (current shape #8): make it central, tighten the cue rule

Current skill has it as one of eight shapes. Promote it and add the cue rule:

> A flat narrator claim + an animation cue in `[brackets]` that contradicts/oversells it. This is a primary engine, not an occasional move. **The cue must be LITERAL, observable physical action** — spinning, trembling, panting, "sits, stares, vibrates." Never a figurative flourish ("achieving liftoff") and never an inferred inner state ("making plans," "telling everyone"). Tender moments use this too: land the feeling through the image, not a statement — "Mosey is finally sleeping calmly. [twitching legs in the air, having a dream]".

*Why:* Natan converted ~6 lines INTO this move; figurative/inner-state cues all rejected (R3, R7).

## 4. CHANGE — register-clash / officialese: split the dead version from the live one

The skill currently elevates officialese and "register clash." Findings: the lazy version is dead, the committed version is gold.

- **CUT** the "declined investigation" shape (current shape #3: "No explanation was offered," "No further information is available"). It failed every clean test (0-for-4+).
- **BAN** one-line bureaucratic tags with no payoff ("no comment was issued," "cleared of wrongdoing").
- **KEEP/ADD** `mock-serious`: a committed straight-faced frame (military, detective, tragic) that resolves in a **tight deflating fragment** — "A great misfortune has befallen Mosey. Bath time." Long mock-serious escalation is risky (mostly failed R7); default to the tight 2-beat. Within a committed bit, Mosey may be renamed for the joke ("Target").

*Why:* declined-investigation 0-for-4; long mock-escalation 0-for-3 in R7; tight mock-deflation approved (R6 detectives, R7 bath).

## 5. ADD — Natan's "for no particular reason" micro-move

> State an odd behavior + "for no particular reason" (or "for no reason at all"), with the obvious cause in brackets. The deadpan is in pretending not to know the cause everyone can see: "Mosey is practicing meowing for no particular reason. [after seeing the cat]".

*Why:* Natan-authored (L043).

## 6. CHANGE — the Squirrel war: paranoid dog-logic, not terse threats

> Play the Squirrel war straight and dramatic, as **paranoid dog-logic** that escalates: "Mosey has not seen the squirrel in 4 days. Mosey does not remember making a truce. The enemy must be scheming." Crisp is good; terse throwaway threats ("This will be addressed") fall flat. The "understatement scales with the moment" rule does NOT apply to the Squirrel.

*Why:* Natan-authored (L044); terse Squirrel lines rejected (R3, R7).

## 7. CHANGE — numbers

Current: "Numbers only as comedy precision." Sharpen to:

> Numbers usually get cut. Keep one only when it is load-bearing for the joke or adds real flavor: "stared at Mosey for a full minute," "has not seen the squirrel in 4 days." Clock-times and arbitrary counts die.

*Why:* 4:12 / "eleven minutes" / "6:02 AM" cut; "a full minute" / "4 days" survived.

## 8. CHANGE — warmth & sincerity

> Default is dry deadpan. **Warmth must be earned by a concrete world-thing** — a place, an object, a promise ("the Good Bench... there's always a friend waiting"). Free-floating warmth is saccharine and gets cut. **Never state the user-bond** ("Mosey missed you," "Mosey knows you are home" — all rejected); show it through behavior/structure. Genuine sincerity stays rare (a few times a year, per the bible) — do not manufacture everyday sincere lines; they consistently fail.

*Why:* R2/R3/R7 — stated-bond and manufactured-sincere lines 0-for-many; earned-place warmth approved.

## 9. ADD — traps to avoid (new short list)

> - "You do X → Mosey already did Y" anticipation at the door — consistently flat.
> - The homecoming/"you are home" beat — weak even when shown.
> - Three-beat restatement (same idea three ways) — boring. Escalation is fine ONLY when each beat adds new information.
> - Field-report/"outings: one, distance: modest" register as a live spawned line.

*Why:* each failed multiple times.

## 10. RESOLVED — the pronoun rule (#1 hard rule) stays STRICT

Natan's decision (final): keep it strict — Mosey is always "Mosey," never he/she/they/it. **No change to this rule.** When repetition threatens to get clunky, the fix is one of: use "Mosey's" (idioms — "In Mosey's dreams"), restructure to remove the need ("Mosey had already eaten, but forgot"), or drop the possessive ("practicing meowing"). Note: the rule governs MOSEY only — other residents (the Snail, etc.) may keep their pronouns. Five banked lines (L012, L014, L040, L041, L043) have been conformed.
