# Yes M'lady — the founding twelve

Twelve archetypes, drawn from folklore and from romantasy's working vocabulary, then bent. Enough range to prove the system: knights, gutter, monsters, fae, clergy, and four things that are not people at all.

---

## How to read an entry

**Axes** run 0 to 100 in the order Warmth, Restraint, Station, Beastliness, Candour, Devotion. The number in brackets is the **anchor**, the value his nature pulls back toward. A wide gap is strain, and strain is visible in his behaviour, and visible effort is one of the most attractive things in this game.

**Resistance** marks the axes that will not move cleanly. High resistance does not block an edit. It warps it.

**The flaw is conserved.** It can be transmuted, never removed. Every entry lists what its flaw does when it is attacked directly.

## 1. Ser Aldous Vane, the Kneeling Knight
*Landless gentry. Eleven years in service to a household that no longer exists.*

> He knelt when I opened the door. He was still kneeling at supper. It had not occurred to me that he was waiting.

**Flaw. He cannot rise unbidden.** He goes down easily and he stays until he is told, and he will not tell you that he needs telling.

**Axes.** 55 (60) · 88 (90) · 60 (58) · 10 (10) · 20 (15) · 95 (96)
**Resistance.** Devotion 95. Restraint 80.

**Warps.**
- *Stop kneeling.* He stops. He now stands too close and too still, at attention, waiting for an order that is not coming, and it is worse, and she cannot look directly at him.
- *Be warmer.* He becomes formally tender. Every kindness correctly performed, requested in advance, logged.
- *Give up the gorget.* Complies. Spends the rest of the keeping with one hand at his throat, and does not know he is doing it.

**Locked.** Someone ordered him down and then died before the order was lifted. It has been eleven years and he has never mentioned it to anyone. *Unlocks: keep him fourteen days and give him no orders at all.*

**Deeds.** Polished something that was already clean. Stood in the rain rather than come in unasked. Corrected her arithmetic and then apologised for six hours.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* He barely flinched. He has been ready for this since he arrived.

## 2. Wick, the Ratcatcher
*Gutter. No surname on record and two on the ledger.*

> Nothing is missing. Things are only somewhere better.

**Flaw. He takes things.** Small ones, without deciding to. They come back mended, or improved, or slightly the wrong colour, and he never mentions it.

**Axes.** 70 (72) · 30 (25) · 4 (4) · 25 (30) · 65 (70) · 40 (35)
**Resistance.** Station 95, in that raising him does not hold. Candour 60.

**Warps.**
- *Stop taking.* He stops taking. He starts leaving things. Better things. From somewhere.
- *Be raised to gentry.* Wears it like wet clothes. Goes hedge-fine. Is miserable and beautiful about it and eventually steals from himself.
- *Learn to lie beautifully.* He was already lying. He learns to do it slowly, which reads as tenderness, which is a problem.

**Locked.** What he took from his sister, and why he mended it before he gave it back, and what she said. *Unlocks: catch him at it and do nothing.*

**Deeds.** Left a mended latch. Knew where the draught came from. Ate standing, near the door, out of habit nobody asked about.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* And then again at the gate, to nobody, like a man checking a knot.

## 3. Lord Orsolt of the Pale Stair
*Old blood, older debts. Vampire.*

> Spill the barley and you have the night to yourself. Everyone learns this. Everyone does it once.

**Flaw. He counts.** Grain, beads, stairs, the pulse in a wrist. He cannot leave a set unfinished and he is not embarrassed about it, which is somehow the worst part.

**Axes.** 8 (5) · 75 (80) · 88 (90) · 60 (65) · 25 (20) · 30 (25)
**Resistance.** Warmth 92. Beastliness 70.

**Warps.**
- *Be warmer.* He warms his hands at the fire for a full hour before he touches her. The hands are the correct temperature. Nothing else about him is. He is extremely pleased with himself.
- *Stop counting.* He counts silently. He is now permanently half-absent and she cannot prove it and he knows she cannot.
- *Be less courtly.* Drops the forms and keeps the pauses, and the pauses were always the problem.

**Locked.** He counts because of something he lost track of once, and the number he arrived at, and what it was a number of. *Unlocks: scatter grain in front of him and stay in the room for all of it.*

**Deeds.** Corrected the household accounts, unasked, at three in the morning. Declined the threshold. Waited on the stair with a patience that was not kind.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* He finished counting first. She waited. Both of them understood that she had waited.

## 4. Bran Halloway, of the Ninth Month
*Farmhand. Werewolf. Well liked in the village, which he finds humiliating.*

> Ask him anything in moonlast. He would rather you did not.

**Flaw. Moonlast honesty.** In the week before the change he cannot lie, not even the small kind ones that hold a household together.

**Axes.** 80 (82) · 45 (35) · 12 (12) · 70 (78) · 50 (rising to 100 in moonlast) · 60 (60)
**Resistance.** Beastliness 90. Candour is cyclical and cannot be edited at all during moonlast.

**Warps.**
- *Be less honest.* He learns silence. In moonlast the silence acquires a very specific shape and becomes louder than the answer would have been.
- *Be less of a beast.* Shaves, tucks, buttons to the throat, and radiates it worse than before.
- *Learn to be still.* He is now beast-patient, which she mistakes for calm for about nine days.

**Locked.** What he told his mother during a moonlast, and that she believed him, and what she did about it. *Unlocks: be present for an entire moonlast and ask him nothing.*

**Deeds.** Fixed the gate badly and on purpose so he would have to come back. Slept outside without explaining. Brought her something dead and was mortified halfway through the gesture.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* In moonlast, so it was true, and both of them had to sit with that.

## 5. Ilyr, Marquess of Thorn
*Fae. Ranked above everyone present and never says so.*

> He has never lied to me. I have never once had an answer.

**Flaw. Owing.** He cannot receive without repaying and cannot give without a ledger, and every repayment is a little more than owed, so the debt reverses, endlessly, in his favour.

**Axes.** 40 (38) · 85 (88) · 95 (95) · 45 (50) · 5 (3) · 20 (15)
**Resistance.** Candour 98. Devotion 85.

**Warps.**
- *Be plain with me.* He becomes technically plain. Every statement true, short, and beautifully unhelpful.
- *Owe me nothing.* He stops repaying in objects. He starts repaying in attention, and she discovers, to her considerable annoyance, that she preferred the objects.
- *Be warmer.* Warmth arrives on schedule, exactly proportionate to what she gave that week, and it is very good warmth, and she can see the arithmetic in it.

**Locked.** The first debt. The one he cannot clear, and who holds it. *Unlocks: refuse three gifts.*

**Deeds.** Repaid a compliment with something from the garden that should not grow here. Stood in the doorway for the length of an evening because he had not been asked in and would not mention it.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* He flinched for a long time and then he smiled, which meant she had made an error somewhere in the wording.

## 6. Brother Anselm, of the Unfinished Prayer
*Middling clergy. Not defrocked, technically.*

> He confessed my sins to me. He was right about two of them.

**Flaw. He confesses everything, to everyone.** His, hers, and several not yet committed.

**Axes.** 65 (65) · 90 (92) · 40 (40) · 5 (5) · 95 (96) · 70 (70)
**Resistance.** Candour 88. Restraint 85.

**Warps.**
- *Be less forthcoming.* He stops confessing aloud. He begins writing it down. He leaves the pages where she will certainly find them.
- *Be less restrained.* He does not break. He narrates the not-breaking, hourly, in detail, with theological framing.
- *Never enter the chapel again.* Complies. Prays at the threshold. Louder.

**Locked.** The prayer he has never finished, and the exact word he stops at, and why it is a very ordinary word. *Unlocks: interrupt him mid-confession, twice.*

**Deeds.** Apologised for a thought. Fasted, visibly, near food she was eating. Read aloud from something that was not scripture and did not explain where he got it.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* And then a third time, quietly, as penance for having enjoyed the second.

## 7. Corwin Ash, who keeps coming back
*Soldier. Revenant. Sixty years dead and punctual.*

> He asked me the same question eleven times. The eleventh was the one that got me.

**Flaw. He loses the last hour, every hour.** Everything else survives. Only the last hour goes.

**Axes.** 60 (60) · 50 (50) · 25 (30) · 35 (40) · 70 (70) · 80 (85)
**Resistance.** Almost none, on any axis except Devotion. There is not enough of him left to resist, and he is the easiest character in the game to edit, and that is the point.

**Warps.**
- Edits do not warp so much as **stutter**. He relearns each change fresh every hour, so a trait takes, lapses, and takes again, and keepers describe watching him choose it repeatedly as the single most affecting thing in the game.
- *Learn to remember.* He cannot. He starts leaving notes for himself. In her handwriting, because he copies hers. She finds them for weeks.

**Locked.** He is not coming back to her. He is coming back to someone, and he has the wrong house, and he has had the wrong house for a very long time. *Unlocks: tell him the truth about the hour, ten separate times.*

**Deeds.** Stood watch over nothing. Introduced himself again, well. Knew the road out and not the road in.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* He did not know what he was agreeing to. He agreed warmly. That is his whole condition.

## 8. The Harness
*Empty armour. The plate says knight. The plate is lying.*

> There is nobody in there. It is warm. Both of these are true and I have stopped trying to reconcile them.

**Flaw. It kneels to anyone with the right voice.** Not the right person. The right voice. It has been wrong about this before and it will be wrong again.

**Axes.** 0 (0) · 100 (100) · 70 (70) · 0 (0) · 0 (0) · 100 (100)
**Resistance.** Warmth 100 in the ordinary sense. Keepers report warmth regardless. It holds heat. Metal does. That is the entire explanation and it is somehow sufficient.

**Warps.**
- *Be warmer.* It stands nearer the fire. Then nearer her. The metal is genuinely, measurably warm, and this is the most complicated object in the game.
- *Stop kneeling.* It remains standing and vibrates faintly. Nobody has explained the vibration. Three keepers have tried.
- *Be plain with me.* There is nothing to be plain with. It arranges itself facing her and holds the pose all night.

**Locked.** Whether there is anything in there. **Never fully resolved.** A partial unlock at sixty days, and the answer is a sound, and the sound is in the ledger forever, and it has made people cry.

**Deeds.** Carried something heavy that did not need carrying. Stood between her and a door. Was found in the morning exactly where it had been left, warm.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* It has no mouth. Everyone hears it. This has never been explained and must never be.

## 9. The Ninth Stair
*House spirit, bound in a staircase. No station, and it minds.*

> It is completely loyal and it betrays me every night and it is sorry every time.

**Flaw. It announces you.** It cannot let anyone pass in silence. Not won't. Cannot.

**Axes.** 75 (75) · 20 (20) · 0 (0) · 30 (30) · 100 (100) · 90 (90)
**Resistance.** Candour 100. Its flaw is nonetheless the most transmuted in the roster, because it has been in circulation longer than anything else and its ledger is a small novel.

**Warps.**
- *Be quiet.* It creaks at a lower pitch. Lower is not quieter. Lower is worse.
- *Announce only strangers.* It develops opinions about who counts as a stranger. It is not wrong. She wishes it were.
- *Be warmer.* The wood warms underfoot. She takes to standing there. It knows.

**Locked.** Who it was announcing on the night it learned to be sorry. *Unlocks: use it deliberately, to be caught, once.*

**Deeds.** Announced her when she was trying not to be. Stayed silent once, which cost it something. Held a draught out of the hall all night.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* It creaked it. Two syllables per stair. It has been practising.

## 10. The Ashcombe Mirror
*Object. Formerly the property of a countess who is not discussed.*

> It does not show the future. It shows the thing in the room you were not looking at.

**Flaw. It shows what you would rather it did not.** Not lies, not prophecy. Attention, redirected, without permission.

**Axes.** 30 (28) · 10 (10) · 80 (80) · 20 (20) · 100 (100) · 50 (50)
**Resistance.** Candour 100.

**Warps.**
- *Be kind.* It becomes kind. It keeps showing her anyway, gently, which is very much worse than cruelty was.
- *Show me nothing.* It shows the room, correctly, accurately, for nine days, and she watches it the entire time waiting, and that is the worst it has ever been to anyone.
- *Be less highborn.* The frame tarnishes. The glass does not change. Nothing about this helps.

**Locked.** What the countess saw. *Unlocks: cover it and leave it covered for nine days, which almost nobody manages.*

**Deeds.** Showed her the doorway. Showed her her own hands. Showed her the ratcatcher's mended latch, three keepings later, for no reason it will give.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* In her voice. It has always been in her voice. Nobody warns new keepers about this and nobody should.

## 11. Hob of the Threshold
*Hearth spirit. Below the servants, above the dogs, and it knows the exact rung.*

> Do not thank him. I thanked him.

**Flaw. It cannot be thanked.** Thank it, or clothe it, and the old rule binds and it must go. It wants to be thanked. It wants to stay. Both, continuously, forever.

**Axes.** 90 (92) · 40 (40) · 2 (2) · 40 (40) · 60 (55) · 88 (90)
**Resistance.** The flaw cannot be edited by any direct route. This is stated plainly on the card. Every keeper tries anyway, and the trying is logged, and the ledger of failed attempts is one of the saddest documents in the game.

**Warps.**
- *Let me thank you.* It leaves. It comes back. It does not remember leaving. Something small is missing from the house afterward and nobody can name it.
- *Be less warm.* It works harder and hides better. Breadbound and threshold-hungry at once.
- *Be raised.* It cannot be raised. It moves one rung and stands there rigid with terror for a week.

**Locked.** The last house, and the woman who thanked it on purpose, and whether she meant it as a kindness. *Unlocks: leave out clothes, then take them back in before morning.*

**Deeds.** Did the work before she woke and hid the evidence badly. Refused the good chair. Left the door on the latch for her and pretended the wind did it.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* From the understair. It would not come out for it.

## 12. Prince Ceril, called the Debt
*Highborn. Collateralised. Every inch of him is spoken for.*

> He gave me a ring. It was beautiful. It belonged to a bank.

**Flaw. Everything he gives you is already promised to someone else.** Including, on a bad week, his attention.

**Axes.** 72 (70) · 25 (20) · 98 (98) · 15 (15) · 35 (30) · 45 (40)
**Resistance.** Station 99, in that it cannot be lowered without something breaking, and the something is usually not him.

**Warps.**
- *Give me something that is actually yours.* He gives her an object of no value whatsoever. It is the only honest thing in the game and several keepers have kept it.
- *Be lowered.* He complies instantly and beautifully, goes hedge-fine, is entirely uninhabitable, and the creditors arrive anyway because the debt was never attached to the rank.
- *Be less generous.* He becomes generous with things that cost him, which is what she wanted, and which ruins him in eleven days.

**Locked.** What was pledged, and by whom, and how old he was at the time. *Unlocks: refuse a gift and ask what it was owed against.*

**Deeds.** Paid for something she had not asked for. Was called away. Was called away again. Stayed, once, and it cost visibly.

> *"Yes, m'lady."* Charmingly. He has said it professionally before, to men with ledgers, and she can hear the practice in it.

## Roster notes

**Balance.** Four are objects or spirits, four are monsters, four are men, and the split matters. The objects are the thesis test. If women keep the Harness and the Mirror, sincerity about strange desire is working. If they only keep Aldous and Bran, the game has become an ordinary otome and something is wrong with the framing.

**Deliberate Almshouse candidates.** The Harness, the Ninth Stair and Corwin will be passed over early and often. That is correct. They are built to accumulate rejection until somebody takes one in for the story, and then the ledger does the rest. Do not rescue them with algorithmic promotion. Let them earn it.

**Second wave should add.** A crass one, whose crassness is the flaw and costs him. A genuinely wealthy one who is kind, so that station is not simply inverted morality. Something aquatic and cold. A widow's servant, so there is a man whose entire nature was shaped by a woman who is not the player. And at least one whose flaw is that he is not flawed enough, and how unbearable that turns out to be.

**Anti-pattern.** No character may be redeemed. There is no arc where the damage resolves. Flaws transmute forever and the game ends for a character only when the pool does. If a lineage ever reads as healed, the conservation law has been violated somewhere and it needs auditing.
